The Harada Method

A time-honoured system for turning one great ambition into daily action

The Four Pillars

I

Name one core goal that truly matters to you.

II

Choose eight sub-goals that would make it real.

III

Write eight actions for every sub-goal.

IV

Practise daily and review your progress.

Your Open Window 64 Chart

Write in the red centre first, then the gold sub-goals. Everything saves automatically on this device.

Counsel for the Journey

  • Make actions small, concrete and measurable — “run 3 km”, not “get fitter”.
  • Balance skill, body, mind and character across your sub-goals.
  • Review your chart weekly; refine what is not working.
