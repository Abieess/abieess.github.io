    04 SOS — When the Urge Hits

    Do not negotiate with the urge. Negotiation is how it wins. Press the button, follow the 90-second protocol, and let the wave pass. Urges peak in 10–15 minutes and always fall.

    1. STOP. Put the phone face-down across the room. Physically move away from the trigger.
    2. BREATHE. Box breathing — inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s, hold 4s. Four rounds.
    3. NAME IT. Say out loud: "This is an urge. It is not me. It will pass."
    4. MOVE. 20 push-ups, a cold wash, or a 5-minute walk. Change the physical state.
    5. DELAY 20 MINUTES. If the urge returns after 20 minutes, run the protocol again. It will weaken each round.
    6. REDIRECT. Open Tamil or aptitude questions. Give your brain a real task — idle brains relapse.

    05 The Relapse Rule

    If you slip, follow this — exactly

    1. Close the tab, put the phone down, and step away for 60 seconds.
    2. Do not binge to "finish the streak properly." That is the chaser effect, and it is a lie.
    3. Reset the counter. Write one sentence in a notebook: what triggered it, what you'll change tomorrow.
    4. Run the SOS protocol once more, immediately. Do not wait for the next urge.
    5. Return to study the same day. Not tomorrow. Today.
    No shame clause. A relapse is a data point, not an identity. The people who succeed are not the ones who never slip — they are the ones whose slips last hours instead of weeks. The exam is in January. You are allowed many resets; you are not allowed to quit.
